What changed in the method this morning

  • Partial days are not scored. Oct 3 (the phone looped 3.5 h, TDD 7.6 U) alone had made 68 of 96 aggressive variants infeasible through the 15 %-of-TDD rule. A day the phone looped less than 16 h is now listed with its values but left out of every verdict. That removes 10 of 42 days (Sep 10, 20, 24, 25; Oct 3, 5, 9, 14, 15, 16). The search set is now 8 days: Oct 4, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13.
  • Envelope-limited is its own verdict. A variant that passes every safety check but moves more than 1 U in a 3 h window on some days is listed with those days instead of being hidden among the infeasible ones.
  • An "earlier" step must beat the noise floor on rises; otherwise it is a tie. No negative step is ever printed as a step.

Hindcast replays the loop's decisions under other settings over the glucose and insulin history that the phone's own settings produced. It says where and how much the loop would have asked differently; it cannot say what glucose would have done. A variant that boluses earlier is charged for its bolus and for the later ones the phone actually gave, so deltas overstate aggressive settings and understate gentle ones. The base replay disagrees with the phone in about 4 % of loops; differences smaller than that floor are ties. Nothing here changes the phone; settings are for you and your care team to review. This is one person's insulin dosing, not medical advice.
