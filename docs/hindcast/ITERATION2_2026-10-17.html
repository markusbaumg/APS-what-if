What changed in the method

  • Pre-hypo rule. A low that starts within 4 h after one of your manual boluses no longer counts against a variant. Lows the loop produced on its own keep the old limit. On the 8 search days this leaves out 6 of 18 low episodes (Oct 4 16:24, 17:41, 18:52 after your 2 U + 1.5 U; Oct 10 16:14 and two re-entries after your 3 U). Still counted: Oct 4 09:15 (no rise, no bolus), Oct 6 13:29 and 15:19, Oct 7 11:21, Oct 8 01:03 and 03:52, Oct 10 08:10 and 18:33. The rule is printed on every page and card.
  • Partial days are not scored. A day the phone looped less than 16 h is listed but left out of every verdict; 10 of 42 days. The search set is 8 days: Oct 4, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13.
  • Envelope-limited is its own verdict, with the days listed; an "earlier" step must beat the noise floor on rises.
  • All seven earlier studies were re-replayed under the new rule (5 500 trial rows, 75 min).

Hindcast replays the loop's decisions under other settings over the glucose and insulin history that the phone's own settings produced. It says where and how much the loop would have asked differently; it cannot say what glucose would have done. A variant that boluses earlier is charged for its bolus and for the later ones the phone actually gave, so deltas overstate aggressive settings and understate gentle ones. The base replay disagrees with the phone in about 4 % of loops; differences smaller than that floor are ties. Nothing here changes the phone; settings are for you and your care team to review. This is one person's insulin dosing, not medical advice.
